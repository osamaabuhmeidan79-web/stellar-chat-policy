Common questions

I did not get the SMS code

Check that the number is right and includes the country code, wait a minute, then tap "Resend". Some networks delay the code. If it never arrives, write to us with your number's country and we will look.

Can you read my messages?

No. Personal chats and groups are end-to-end encrypted: they are locked on your phone and opened only on the phones in the conversation. We store and deliver the locked envelopes and cannot open them. See the Privacy Policy.

I lost my phone. Can you restore my chats?

Only from a backup you made; the locked messages on our servers cannot be opened by us. Sign in on the new phone with the same number to keep your account, contacts and groups.

Calls do not ring

Allow notifications for Stellar Chat in your phone's settings, and make sure the app is not restricted in the background (battery saver on Android). If it still happens, tell us the time of the missed call and we will check it.

Someone is bothering me

Open their profile and tap Block; they can no longer message or call you. Tap Report so we see it. For anything serious, write to us.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account → Delete account. Your profile, messages, posts and media are removed as described in the Privacy Policy.
