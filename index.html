Posts, stories and activity

  • Videos and shorts you post, with their title and caption, and the approximate location they were posted from (or the business's location, if you tag one), so they can appear in "Near me" feeds. Other users see an approximate distance, not the location itself.

Business bookings and conversations

If you message a business or book with it, the conversation and the booking (service, time, the name and phone number given for it, and any note) are stored and shared with that business and its staff. Messages to a business may be answered by its AI front desk and translated between languages — see "Who else receives data" below.

Content you send

Messages, voice notes, photos, videos, documents, and locations you choose to share are stored so they can be delivered and so you can see them again later. Media files are stored with our hosting provider; message records are stored in our database. In personal chats and groups they are stored as locked envelopes and locked files that only the phones in the conversation can open.

Personal chats and groups are end-to-end encrypted. Messages, voice notes, photos, videos and documents in personal chats and groups are locked on your phone with keys that only the phones in the conversation hold, and opened only on those phones. Our servers store and deliver the locked envelopes but cannot read them, and we keep no way to. One-to-one calls are end-to-end encrypted between the two phones. A group call is end-to-end encrypted when every member's app supports it; the call screen shows a lock, or a warning when it does not. Chats with Stellar AI, a business's AI front desk, posts, stories, Flicks and comments are encrypted between your phone and our servers and are readable by us as described below. If you lose your phone and have no encrypted backup, your locked messages cannot be recovered by us.

Information collected automatically

  • A push notification token (from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, or Apple's push service on iPhone), so we can alert you to messages and calls.
  • Your approximate location, sent with requests for the "Near me" video feed and for nearby businesses while you use them, to find what is close to you. It is used to answer that request, and is only stored when you post a video (see above).
  • Whether you are currently online, shown to your contacts.
  • Your muted, blocked, favourite and locked chat lists.
  • Basic technical information needed to deliver messages and calls.
  • Anonymous call performance data — during voice and video calls, the app records every 20 seconds how warm the phone is (its heat level), how much processor and memory Stellar Chat uses, and call quality figures (video size, frame rate, connection quality), with the phone model, system version and app version. It is sent once when the call ends, is not linked to your account, name or phone number, contains nothing you said or showed, and is used only to make calls work better. It is deleted after 30 days.

How contacts are used. To find which of your contacts use Stellar Chat, and to let people whose numbers you have saved start a chat with you, the app uploads the phone numbers in your address book (and, on Android, on your SIM card) as one-way SHA-256 hashes — not as readable numbers. Names, photos, emails and other contact details are not uploaded. The app explains this and asks before it requests the contacts permission; you can choose "Not now" or refuse the permission and still use Stellar Chat, though people who have your number will then need to follow you before they can message you. When you share a single contact in a chat, only that contact's name and number are sent, as part of the message. The hashes are replaced each time the app syncs, and deleted with your account.

How long we keep it

  • Messages and media — until you delete them, or until you delete your account.
  • Account details — for as long as your account exists.
  • Anonymous call performance data — 30 days.
  • Deleted account data — removed from our live systems within 30 days. Backups may retain it for up to 90 days before being overwritten.

Deleting your account

You can delete your Stellar Chat account and everything in it at any time.

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account.
  • By email: write to the address at the top of this policy from the email on your account, or tell us the phone number registered to it.

Deleting removes your profile, your push token, your uploaded contact hashes, your videos, stories and highlights, your comments and likes, your follows, your bookings and business conversations, any business you own, and your one-to-one conversations. Media files you uploaded are no longer linked to you or shown anywhere in the app; if you also want the stored files themselves erased from our media host, email us. Because a conversation is stored once and shared by both people rather than copied to each, deleting your account also removes those conversations from the other person's chat list.

Groups you belonged to carry on without you, and messages you posted in a group are deleted. Reports other people have made about you are kept, with the reporter's identity detached, so that safety records survive.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how it is used, or ask for it in a portable format. To exercise any of these, email the address above. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the UK or EU and are unhappy with our response, you may complain to your national data protection authority.

Children

Stellar Chat is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

Personal chats and groups are end-to-end encrypted, as described above. All data is encrypted in transit. Passwords are hashed and never stored in a readable form. Access to our systems is limited. No service can promise perfect security, and we have described above what this app does and does not protect against.

International transfers

Our providers operate servers in several countries, so your data may be processed outside the country you live in. We use providers that offer appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
