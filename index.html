Content you send

Messages, voice notes, photos, videos, documents, and locations you choose to share are stored so they can be delivered and so you can see them again later. Media files are stored with our hosting provider; message records are stored in our database.

Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. They are encrypted in transit, and access to our servers is restricted, but we are technically able to access message content. Please do not use Stellar Chat for information that requires end-to-end encryption.

Information collected automatically

  • A push notification token, so we can alert you to messages and calls.
  • Whether you are currently online, shown to your contacts.
  • Your muted, blocked, favourite and locked chat lists.
  • Basic technical information needed to deliver messages and calls.

How long we keep it

  • Messages and media — until you delete them, or until you delete your account.
  • Account details — for as long as your account exists.
  • Deleted account data — removed from our live systems within 30 days. Backups may retain it for up to 90 days before being overwritten.

Deleting your account

You can delete your Stellar Chat account and everything in it at any time.

  • In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • By email: write to the address at the top of this policy from the email on your account, or tell us the phone number registered to it.

Deleting removes your profile, your uploaded media, your push token, and your one-to-one conversations. Because a conversation is stored once and shared by both people rather than copied to each, deleting your account also removes those conversations from the other person's chat list.

Groups you belonged to carry on without you, and messages you posted in a group are deleted. Reports other people have made about you are kept, with the reporter's identity detached, so that safety records survive.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how it is used, or ask for it in a portable format. To exercise any of these, email the address above. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the UK or EU and are unhappy with our response, you may complain to your national data protection authority.

Children

Stellar Chat is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Passwords are hashed and never stored in a readable form. Access to our systems is limited. No service can promise perfect security, and we have described above what this app does and does not protect against.

International transfers

Our providers operate servers in several countries, so your data may be processed outside the country you live in. We use providers that offer appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
